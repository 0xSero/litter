privacy policy

last updated: march 23, 2026

Overview

Litter ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile client that connects to Codex servers you control. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Do Not Collect

Litter does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our servers. Specifically:

  • We do not collect analytics or usage telemetry.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not read your contacts or scan your photo library. Photos, videos, files, or camera frames you explicitly select or capture are used only for the conversation, wallpaper, or QR-pairing action you request.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.

Data That Stays on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Server credentials: SSH keys, authentication tokens, and server connection details are stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences).
  • App preferences: Theme, text size, and other settings are stored locally.

Network Communication

Litter communicates directly with Codex servers that you configure. This communication happens:

  • Over a local or private network for server endpoints you add.
  • Over SSH tunnels for remote server connections.
  • Over Alleycat peer-to-peer or relay connections when you choose QR pairing.

Conversation content, code, and session data for a configured server flow between your device and that server. Encrypted Alleycat traffic may transit a selected relay, and optional voice processing is described below. We do not operate a service that inspects or stores this traffic.

Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, a device token is registered with our push notification relay service. This token is used solely to deliver notifications and is not linked to any personal information. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Voice Input

When you choose voice input, audio is sent from your device to OpenAI for transcription or real-time voice processing. Realtime signaling, transcripts, and session coordination also pass through the Codex or Local Studio server you configure. We do not operate these voice-processing endpoints, and we do not store or access your audio recordings.

Third-Party Services

Litter does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Depending on the features you choose, the app communicates with server connections you configure, OpenAI voice and transcription services, Alleycat relay infrastructure you pair through, and our push notification relay.

Children's Privacy

Litter is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

Since we do not collect your conversation data, there is no conversation archive to delete from our servers. Remove saved servers and credentials in the app before uninstalling. Uninstalling removes the app container, but operating-system secure storage such as iOS Keychain may follow separate retention rules.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at privacy@sigkitten.com.
