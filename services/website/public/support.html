support

need help with litter? here's how to reach us.

email

For general questions, bug reports, or feedback, email us at support@sigkitten.com.

common issues

Can't connect to my server? Verify that the endpoint you added is reachable from your device, or configure an SSH connection for remote access.

local data

Litter does not create accounts or keep a conversation archive on our servers. Use the in-app controls to remove saved connections. Uninstalling removes the app container, but operating-system secure storage such as iOS Keychain may follow separate retention rules.
